Keys

Q / A forward / back (or cursor up / down), O / P turn (or cursor left / right), Z / X strafe, SPACE fire, E or ENTER use. SPACE or ENTER starts on the title screen. A gamepad works as joystick 1.

K saves the game, L loads it (also on the title and the banners). Border: white = saved, yellow = loaded, magenta = failed. The save stays in this browser.

Needs a keyboard (there are no touch controls). Click the picture first. If the page shows an error about WebGL, your browser or its graphics settings do not provide WebGL 2, which the emulator needs.
